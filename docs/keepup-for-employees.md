# Keepup: a quick guide

Keepup is where you track your work and log updates. Open it at **keepup.devdeepsaha.in**.

## Getting started
1. Open the invite email and click the link.
2. Enter your name and choose a password.
3. From then on, sign in with your email and that password.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email.

## Your spaces
- **Mint-more:** your office work. Your managers and founders may be able to see this.
- **Personal:** your own to-dos. **Only you can see it.** Spaces you create yourself are private too.
- **Bouncy:** the assistant in the corner. Tell it what you did ("sent the Tomboy homepage") and it logs it for you. Your chats with Bouncy are private.

## Roles
| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Everyone. Tracks their own work, and accepts or declines tasks given to them. |
| **Manager** | Sees the Mint-more work of the people a founder gives them. May also edit it and assign tasks. |
| **Founder** | Sees the Mint-more work of the people the admin gives them. Can assign tasks and appoint managers. |
| **Admin** | Invites people and decides who can see whom. |

Nobody can see your Personal space or your Bouncy chats. That includes the admin, founders and managers.

## Daily use
- **Add a task:** type it in the box at the top of Mint-more.
- **Log an update:** open a task and write what happened. Only you can log on your tasks.
- **Needs attention:** this section lists tasks that are late, or where a client check-in was missed.
- **On hold:** waiting on the client? Open the task, tap **Edit**, then **On hold?**. It moves to On hold straight away.
- **On a phone:** tap **⋯** on a task (or press and hold it) for all its actions. In Bouncy, Enter starts a new line; tap the **↑** button to send.
- **Planned client updates:** messages ready to send on certain days. Copy one, send it, then tap **Mark sent**.

## Tasks given to you
- Tasks someone assigns you appear under **New from your team**.
- **Accept** adds the task to your list.
- **Decline** asks for a short reason, which the person who assigned it will see.

## The bell 🔔
The bell at the top shows when someone changes or assigns your work. For example, "Arjun moved the due date to Friday."

## Questions?
Ask Bouncy ("what's due today?"), or ask the admin.
